Skip it, your way.

Skipz marks the sponsor part of a YouTube video, names the brand, and skips it for you. It asks first, unless you tell it not to. It works on videos nobody has marked yet, and nothing leaves your computer.

Get it on GitHub Browsers

The real Skipz on a stand-in video page: the mark, the question, the skip, and Undo.

How it decides

Skipz marks a sponsor only when it is sure enough. It combines four signs, all from the page you already have open.

The paid label

YouTube's own "includes paid promotion" badge.

The description

Sponsor lines, links and deal codes.

The chapters

A chapter named after the brand.

The captions

Spoken ad reads, like "this video is sponsored by", with their times.

It starts the mark two seconds early and ends it three seconds late, then asks before skipping. Prefer not to be asked? Switch that off and it skips by itself, with Undo one tap away.

Private by design

  • No account, no key, no server.
  • It reads the video page you have open, in your browser, and sends nothing anywhere.
  • It asks for three things: storage, scripting, and access to YouTube. Nothing else.
  • The code is open. You can read every rule.
